Surgical instrument carrier robot

Embedded navigation and instrument-delivery software for an eight-person university robotics project.

PROJECT TYPESole software developer within eight-person team
TOOLS & TECHNOLOGIESArduino · C/C++ · IR sensing · Ultrasonic sensing
STATUSUniversity project completed and reviewed

ENGINEERING RECORD

Design and implementation

01

Finite-state controller

Implemented four navigation states covering line following, line recovery, target approach and instrument delivery.

02

Sensor-driven navigation

Used three IR sensors with proportional motor-speed correction, directional memory for line recovery and ultrasonic range sensing for approach.

03

Integration and validation

Built hardware-independent simulation and subsystem tests before final mechanical and electrical integration.

04

Demonstrated outcome

The final 2.041 kg robot passed formal university review and demonstrated line following and delivery within the 300 × 300 × 400 mm design envelope.
