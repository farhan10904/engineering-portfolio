Brayton-cycle gas turbine simulator

A numerical tool comparing ideal and non-ideal Brayton cycles, highlighting the trade-off between net power and thermal efficiency.

PROJECT TYPEPersonal project
TOOLS & TECHNOLOGIESPython · NumPy · Pandas · Matplotlib
STATUSComputational model and parameter sweeps completed

ENGINEERING RECORD

Design and implementation

01

Model assumptions

Implemented compressor efficiency of 85%, turbine efficiency of 90% and combustor pressure loss of 5% in the non-ideal cycle.

02

Parametric study

Swept pressure ratios from 2 to 50 and turbine inlet temperatures from 1400 to 1800 K, calculating net output and first- and second-law efficiencies.

03

Power optimum

At 10 kg/s, the real-cycle model found a maximum net power of 2.73 MW at pressure ratio 10.

04

Efficiency optimum

The maximum thermal efficiency was 38.7% at pressure ratio 23, with 49.2% second-law efficiency at that point.

VERIFICATION

Evidence and limitations

Figures are model outputs under specified assumptions, not measurements from a physical gas turbine. Original plots and source code should be attached before the report is presented as complete.
