ESP32 ultrasonic radar scanner

A servo-driven ultrasonic scanner with a 180° sweep, local OLED output and a live browser-based radar display.

PROJECT TYPEPersonal project
TOOLS & TECHNOLOGIESESP32 · HC-SR04 · SG90 · OLED · Wi-Fi
STATUSPhysical prototype built

ENGINEERING RECORD

Design and implementation

01

System design

Integrated an HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor, SG90 servo, OLED display and live Wi-Fi dashboard.

02

Early simulation

Validated logic in Wokwi before building the physical electronics.

03

Power architecture

Separated servo and ultrasonic supply from the ESP32 using a 5 V MT3608/18650 power rail with common ground.

04

Interface protection

Used a resistor divider to protect the ESP32 3.3 V GPIO from the HC-SR04 5 V Echo signal and debugged sensor/servo timing faults.

VERIFICATION

Evidence and limitations

The prototype and troubleshooting are documented in the CV. Range-error, angular-repeatability and power-consumption measurements have not been supplied.
