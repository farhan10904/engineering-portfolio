ESP32 dual-axis PID gimbal

A two-axis self-levelling platform using an MPU6050 IMU, two MG996R servos and a custom ESP32 carrier PCB.

PROJECT TYPEPersonal project
TOOLS & TECHNOLOGIESESP32 · C/C++ · MPU6050 · KiCad · Fusion 360
STATUSPrototype assembled previously · rebuild and measurement planned

ENGINEERING RECORD

Design and implementation

01

Control architecture

Combined accelerometer and gyroscope readings with a complementary filter. Independent PID loops controlled roll and pitch.

02

Mechanical integration

Designed and modified mounting geometry and electronics enclosure in Fusion 360. PLA components were manufactured by FDM.

03

Custom electronics

Designed and assembled a KiCad carrier PCB with removable ESP32 headers, remote IMU connector, 1000 µF bulk capacitance and a separate 5 V servo rail.

04

Observed limitations

Large movements revealed power-delivery limits and PLA frame flex. Mechanical neutral offsets were calibrated, and the validated operating range was limited to ±5° per axis.

VERIFICATION

Evidence and limitations

Original project documentation records a limited ±5° validated range; quantified settling-time, transient supply-voltage and tracking-error measurements have not yet been added.

Next evidence to add: Reassemble the hardware, inspect wiring, capture baseline response and power measurements, and publish a second report with verified test data.
